Part A — Onboarding process

1. Purpose and scope

This document defines how a new business (“the client”) is taken from signed deal to a live Aj Smart Biz account, and records what that client needs. It makes sure that:

  • the right plan is sold, with the features and limits the client actually needs;
  • everything the platform needs (branding, domain, content, catalogue, staff) is collected once, up front;
  • the client has a working website and company workspace and knows how to use them on day one.

In scope

  • Requirements capture and plan selection
  • Company provisioning and first payment
  • Domain and website design set-up
  • Initial content, catalogue, services and stock
  • Roles, staff accounts and training

Out of scope

  • Custom code or a custom website design for one client
  • Payment gateway, SMS or email integration (not yet available)
  • Data migration beyond what the console screens accept
  • Professional photography and copywriting (optional AI draft only)

4. Onboarding business rules

#Rule
OB-1A company is never provisioned without an agreed plan. A company with no running plan cannot create branches or admins, and its website shows a holding page.
OB-2Only features ticked on the plan can be switched on. If the client needs a feature, choose a plan that grants it — do not promise it otherwise.
OB-3Limits (branches, admins, storage) come from the plan at activation. Changing the plan later does not change a running term; use upgrade instead.
OB-4The generated main-admin password is shown once. Deliver it securely; the client must change it at first sign-in.
OB-5The platform never publishes invented claims. Services, figures, team and testimonials come from the client (or an AI draft the client approves).
OB-6Customer accounts (phone + OTP) are not switched on for live clients until an SMS gateway is connected.
OB-7Every payment is recorded as a transaction with payment mode and reference at provisioning.
OB-8A domain must be owned by the client and pointed to the platform before go-live; branch sub-domains are pinned to their branch.

6. Go-live acceptance criteria

✓Criterion
Company is active; subscription status active with the agreed dates; payment recorded as a transaction.
Main admin has signed in and changed the password.
Website opens on the client's domain over HTTPS, with the correct logo, favicon, colours and design.
Login page of the company workspace shows the client's branding on its domain.
Every feature in 5.4 is switched on and shows “live” (not “Nothing published yet”).
Default slides replaced; no placeholder text left on the website.
Branch domains show branch content (or inherit company content as agreed).
A test enquiry reaches Service Leads and/or WhatsApp as configured.
A test order is created, priced correctly, and moves pending → confirmed → dispatched with stock changing; then cancelled to restore stock.
A test booking appears in the diary and can be accepted.
A test invoice can be issued and printed; then cancelled.
Each staff account signs in and sees only its permitted menus.
All test orders, bookings and invoices are cancelled or marked before handover.
