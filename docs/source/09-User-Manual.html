Part A — Super Admin guide

A1. Create a plan

  1. Go to Plan Management → Add plan.
  2. Enter name, code, price (and discount price), currency, billing cycle, duration and trial days.
  3. Set quotas: max branches, max admins, users, storage MB.
  4. Tick the functionalities the plan grants (website sections, products, orders, warehouse, invoices…).
  5. Choose Public (shown on the product website), Show in admin (tenants may request it) and Popular. Save.
Editing a plan is safeRunning subscriptions keep the terms they were sold (snapshot). Changes apply to new terms only.

A2. Onboard a company

  1. Company Management → New company.
  2. Fill the profile (name, business type, contact, address, GST/PAN), login branding (logo, favicon, description).
  3. Main admin: name, email, phone; leave the password empty to generate one.
  4. Choose the starting plan and record the payment (mode, reference).
  5. Optional: Duplicate from an existing company (website, services, products, blog, testimonials, branches, roles).
  6. Save. Copy the generated admin password now — it is shown only once.
  7. Open the company → Domains and add its website host. Ask DevOps to add the nginx vhost (Document 07).
  8. Optional: AI content creator on the company form drafts slides, About, Contact, benefits, services, products and blog posts. It never overwrites existing content.

A4. Other platform tasks

  • Dashboard — income this month and growth, companies by plan, recent transactions.
  • Customers / Lead Management — read-only, all tenants.
  • Super Admins — add staff (role staff), reset passwords. The root account cannot be removed.
  • Masters — states (with GST code), business types, theme presets.
  • Our website — edit the product site's content, FAQ and template showcase; follow up enquiries (new → contacted → converted/closed); campaign analysis by UTM.

Part B — Company Admin guide

B1. First sign-in

  1. Sign in with the email and password from the platform.
  2. You are taken to Profile to set your own password — required before anything else.
  3. Check Dashboard and My Plan for your plan, days left and limits.

B2. Set up your website

  1. Company Details → Company profile: logo, favicon (.ico), description, contact and address.
  2. Theme: pick primary, secondary and accent colours (for the whole company or one branch).
  3. Functionality: switch on the features your plan includes. A locked switch tells you why (not in plan, expired…). Add WhatsApp numbers by type (inquiry, contact, support, orders) — one contact number is enough to make every button work.
  4. Slider: replace the three default slides with your own (desktop and phone images, button text and link).
  5. Fill each section you switched on: About us, Figures, Team, Gallery, Features/Benefits, Testimonials, Contact page, Social, Blog. A section stays off the website until it has content — the banner says “Nothing published yet”.
  6. Rename pages in the menu with the Menu name field on About, Contact and Services.
Branch websitesUse the Editing for / Shows on selector to give a branch its own About, team, slides or colours. A branch with nothing of its own shows the company-wide content.

B3. Catalogue, services and orders

  1. Categories: build up to three levels (e.g. Furniture → Tables → Dining tables).
  2. Products: name, up to 8 photos (first is the card image), price, optional offer price (discount is calculated), or a free-text price label (“On request”), specifications, stock status, Orderable.
  3. Cart & orders: choose where orders go — WhatsApp, Payment (UPI id or link) or None; basket on/off; required fields; minimum order.
  4. Service categories / Services: cards with photo or icon, “what's included”, price line, duration; make a service bookable with opening hours, slot length and capacity; choose where enquiries go (WhatsApp and Service Leads, Service Leads only, WhatsApp only).

B4. Team access

  1. Role Management → Add role (e.g. “Counter staff”).
  2. Menu Permission: choose the role, tick View/Create/Edit/Delete/Export per menu, save.
  3. Admin Management → Add admin with that role and branch. If the button is disabled, the bar above explains the plan limit and offers Upgrade your plan.

B5. Change plan

My Plan lists plans marked Upgrade or Downgrade. Choose one and send a request with a note. Only one request can be open; you can cancel it. The platform's decision appears on the same screen.

Part C — Staff daily operations

C1. Orders

  1. Orders lists new orders as Pending.
  2. Move each forward: Confirm (stock is reserved) → Packed → Dispatched (stock leaves the warehouse) → Delivered.
  3. Mark payment separately (unpaid / paid / refunded).
  4. Mistake? Cancel (stock goes back) — orders never move backwards and cannot be deleted.
  5. Totals reading “plus N items to be quoted” include products priced in words.
  6. The Analytics tab shows sales for 7/30/90/365 days (cancelled orders excluded).

C2. Warehouse

  • Warehouses: one is the default; others can serve a branch.
  • Movements: record receipts (purchase), returns, damage, and stock counts (enter how many there are now).
  • Transfer stock between warehouses.
  • Stock is never edited directly — corrections are new movements, so the history stays complete.
  • Products with stock tracking show in/out of stock on the website automatically.

C3. Invoices (counter billing)

  1. Invoices → New: add products, quantities, line discounts and tax rate (0/5/12/18/28 %), bill discount.
  2. Save draft to finish later, or Issue — stock is deducted and an invoice number assigned.
  3. Record payment (cash, UPI, card, bank, credit) — partial payments are allowed.
  4. Print the bill from the invoice page. Cancelling an issued invoice returns stock.

C4. Service leads and bookings

  • Each enquiry/booking shows who, which service, and whether they were also sent to WhatsApp — check before calling.
  • Move the stage (new → contacted → qualified → converted / lost) and add notes.
  • Accept or decline booking requests; use the Diary to see a day's bookings.

C5. Customers and leads

  • Customers sign up themselves on the website; you can view spend, addresses and orders, or bar a customer (their orders remain).
  • Lead Management shows website visitors per device with source and pages; set a stage and notes.

Part D — What website visitors see

  • A home page with your slides and every section you have published.
  • Products with photos, prices and offers; Add to cart or Order now, then WhatsApp or payment.
  • Services with Enquire or a booking calendar.
  • Sign in with phone number and a 6-digit code to save addresses and see orders and bookings.
  • If your plan lapses, a branded “back shortly” page replaces the website until it is renewed.
